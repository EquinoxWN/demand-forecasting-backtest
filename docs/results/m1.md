## What the numbers say (Analyze)

- **Weekly seasonality dominates the store total.** Repeating last week (WAPE 0.072) is four times
  better than repeating yesterday (0.295) and twice as good as a flat 28-day mean (0.151).
  Averaging the same weekday over four weeks smooths noise and is the only challenger that counts
  at store level (0.064).
- **Items are noisy and intermittent.** At item level the error is 87% of volume for seasonal
  naive: many items sell 0 to 2 units a day, so copying one week copies its noise. Both averaging
  methods beat it (0.748 and 0.766). This is the bar M2 models must clear per level.
- **MASE above 1 at item level** means even the averages are worse than an in-sample seasonal-naive
  step on average per series; it is reported next to WAPE so volume weighting does not hide this.
- **Only one store was run on M5.** With one store, total equals store. All ten stores as float64
  matrices need several gigabytes, more than was free; multi-store sums are tested on synthetic
  data only.
- **Defects found while building:** the first `mase` raised an error when no series varied, which
  made a perfectly weekly series fail the whole evaluation; it now returns NaN with a series count
  of 0. Three hand mutations survived the first test suite (see the quality check); tests were added.

## Reproduce

- Data: the mirror URL and SHA-256 above (pinned in `data.py`); `make download`.
- Commands: `make test`, `make run`, `make features`. Synthetic data is seeded (`seed=0` in the CLI,
  fixed seeds in tests), so runs are deterministic; feature exports are byte-identical on re-run.
- No model is trained in M1, so no model files exist. The downloaded archive and the exported
  feature file were deleted after the run.
